02 The brief

A jet charter for people who measure life in minutes.

  • On-demand jet charter service, based in Hamburg, Germany
  • High standards of safety, quality and service
  • Expert advice on the choice of plane
  • Competitive prices
Our aim

To target the world’s top business people — executives and VIPs.

  • Remind management what they asked for — the four facts on the left.
  • Main point: top business people don’t buy a seat, they buy time, certainty and privacy.
  • This insight drives every idea we show today.

10 Radio spot · script

“Milan in three hours”

  • Length30 seconds
  • AirtimeMorning business radio, 7–9 am
  • VoiceCalm, warm, deep male or female voice
  • MusicSlow piano with a low bass pulse
  1. SFXBusy airport terminal. Distant announcements. A phone rings.
  2. Announcer“We regret to announce that flight 407 to Milan is delayed…”
  3. Man(stressed) “My board meeting in Milan starts in three hours!”
  4. SFXSudden silence. One low panther growl.
  5. MusicElegant piano and deep bass begin.
  6. Voice-over“There is another way to fly. Panther Air — on-demand jet charter from Hamburg.”
  7. SFXJet engines rise, then a smooth whoosh.
  8. Voice-over“Experts choose the right plane for you. The highest standards of safety and service. Prices that will surprise you.”
  9. Man(relaxed) “Milan? I’ll be early.” SFX: ice in a glass
  10. Voice-over“Panther Air. Your time. Your sky.” Music: final piano chord
  • Introduce yourself: you cover the radio spot, other media and the launch.
  • Explain that radio reaches executives in the car in the morning.
  • Press “Play the script” and read the lines as they light up, or perform it with your team.
  • Point out the contrast: noise and stress, then silence, then calm.
